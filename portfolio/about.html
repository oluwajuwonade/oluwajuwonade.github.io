PROFILE / PERSPECTIVE

Disciplined analysis.
Expansive curiosity.

I am Oluwajuwon Adediji, a Data & Quantitative Analyst with an academic foundation in Economics and a multidisciplinary interest in how evidence, incentives, technology and human behaviour shape decisions.

My central working principle: an analytical output should make the decision clearer, not make the analyst look clever. Methods, assumptions and limitations should be visible enough for another person to inspect the reasoning.

Professional identity

My primary professional identity is Data & Quantitative Analyst. I work across business analytics, quantitative research, economics, financial analysis, business intelligence and AI training/evaluation. These are connected applications of one approach: define the question, inspect the evidence, choose an appropriate method and explain what the result can—and cannot—support.

Working standards

  • Separate source facts, estimates, assumptions and recommendations.
  • Validate definitions, grain, missingness and reconciliations before interpreting metrics.
  • Label synthetic datasets and illustrative scenarios clearly.
  • Use public sources with provenance and observation periods.
  • Do not claim client outcomes, production performance or causal impact without evidence.
