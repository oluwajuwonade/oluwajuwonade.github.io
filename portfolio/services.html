SERVICES / COLLABORATION

Analysis that answers
a real question.

I support teams and independent clients that need better visibility, reliable analysis or a stronger evidence base for decisions. The scope starts with the problem—not a predetermined tool.

01 / BUSINESS ANALYTICS

Performance diagnosis & KPI reporting

Investigate revenue, margin, volume, price, mix, customers, products or channel performance. Establish metric definitions, validate the data and communicate what changed.

  • KPI definitions and performance analysis
  • Trend, segment and driver investigation
  • Executive summary and action priorities
02 / BI & DATA

SQL, reporting layers & dashboards

Structure data and reporting logic so operational and management metrics are more consistent, explainable and reusable.

  • Data preparation and analytical SQL
  • Metric reconciliation and reporting logic
  • Dashboard specifications and visual reporting
03 / QUANTITATIVE RESEARCH

Economic and statistical analysis

Investigate research questions using appropriate quantitative methods, source-aware public data and transparent interpretation.

  • Research design and data sourcing
  • Statistical analysis and modelling
  • Evidence-led briefs and reports
04 / FINANCIAL & COMMERCIAL

Forecasts, scenarios and unit economics

Translate assumptions into modelled outcomes so alternatives, constraints and sensitivities can be compared before action.

  • Forecasting and scenario modelling
  • Break-even, contribution and ROI
  • Model checks and assumption documentation
05 / AI QUALITY

AI training, evaluation & research QA

Apply structured evaluation to AI-assisted outputs and research workflows, with attention to accuracy, completeness, evidence support and error patterns.

  • Evaluation rubrics and scorecards
  • Claim-level evidence checks
  • Failure analysis and quality reporting
06 / COMMUNICATION

Executive reports & presentations

Turn technical work into decision-ready deliverables for managers, founders, researchers and non-technical audiences.

  • Executive summaries and analytical reports
  • PowerPoint presentations
  • Visual explanations and recommendations

Engagement process

01 — Clarify

Define the problem, decision, audience and what a useful answer would look like.

02 — Scope

Agree on required inputs, deliverables, method, timeline, assumptions and exclusions before work begins.

03 — Analyse

Inspect and validate the evidence, conduct the agreed analysis and keep the reasoning reproducible where appropriate.

04 — Deliver

Provide the agreed report, model, dashboard specification or evaluation with limitations and practical next steps.
