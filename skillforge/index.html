    Local-first

    This release stores progress in local browser storage. It does not create an account, sync between devices, or send assessment answers to a server. Export a JSON copy to back up your record. Reset permanently clears this browser's saved SkillForge progress.

    Product & transparency

    About SkillForge

    A practical, independent learning project designed to turn knowledge into demonstrable capability and professional options.

    Design principles

    01
    Evidence over course completionEvery module points to a task or artifact.
    02
    Connect technical skills to business valueLearn to frame problems, communicate outcomes and reach buyers.
    03
    Low-cost by defaultStatic hosting, browser storage and portable exports.
    04
    Honest about limitationsNo false claims about AI automation, earnings or credentials.

    Current release status

    SkillForge currently includes the 28-day curriculum, a rules-based assessment, lesson knowledge checks, local progress tracking, project milestones and exportable learning records.

    Not yet enabled: Live generative AI coaching, learner accounts, cross-device sync, email notifications, payments, admin content management and certificates. These require backend services, security work and/or external credentials. A study-prompt builder is provided as a free interim workflow.

    Built for

    Learners interested in Data Analytics, Business Intelligence, AI workflows, Economics, Quantitative Research and income generation through professional services.
